# I2C pull-up hub — KiCad code generation

Final entry files are `attempt-02/hub.kicad_pro`, `attempt-02/hub.kicad_sch`, and `attempt-02/hub.kicad_pcb`. Open the project in KiCad 10. The schematic, footprints, copper routes, vias, outline, and silkscreen are native editable objects. `attempt-02/generate.py` is the retained editable generation source. The adjacent library tables and `libraries/` directory include the exact standard symbols and footprints used.

J1/J2/J3 use standard 1×4 vertical 2.54 mm through-hole header footprints. All map 1=+3V3, 2=GND, 3=SDA, 4=SCL. C1/C2/C3 are 100 nF in 0805 footprints and sit 3.8 mm from their connector's geometric centre. R1/R2 are 4.7 kohm in 0805 footprints. Their pad 1 connects +3V3; pad 2 connects to the corresponding solder-jumper pad 1. Jumper pad 2 connects SDA or SCL. JP1/JP2 use the standard 1.3 mm pitch open solder-jumper footprint with 1.0×1.5 mm rectangular pads, leaving a 0.30 mm copper gap. Pull-ups are disabled until the user solders the respective jumper.

The outline is 45×35 mm. Copper has two layers with 0.25 mm traces and minimum 0.25 mm clearance rules; vias are 0.60 mm with 0.30 mm drills. Connector centres are 3 mm from their required edges. The generation method used direct native schematic S-expressions with standard embedded symbols and KiCad Python `pcbnew` bindings for the board, with a clearance-aware two-layer grid maze router. No GUI operations, tscircuit, external designs, or evaluator references were used.

# Nine-key diode-isolated matrix

Entry files: `attempt-03/matrix.kicad_pro`, `attempt-03/matrix.kicad_sch`, and `attempt-03/matrix.kicad_pcb`. `generate.py` is the editable generation source. Open native files directly in KiCad. Attempt-01 and attempt-02 are preserved historical candidates, including the initial crossing failure. Native SVG and netlist exports are retained per candidate.

Method: Python generated the schematic S-expression using embedded standard KiCad symbols, and KiCad 10.0.6 pcbnew bindings generated the placed/routed two-layer board. No GUI, tscircuit, reference design, or external benchmark artifacts were used. The libraries used are bundled KiCad standard libraries; subsets are included for portability.

Components: nine generic normally-open 6 mm tactile switches with the standard `Button_Switch_THT:SW_PUSH_6mm` footprint. Its two pad-1 terminals form one electrical terminal and its two pad-2 terminals form the other; both copies are explicitly routed on the board. Diodes are 1N4148 in DO-35, using `Diode_THT:D_DO-35_SOD27_P7.62mm_Horizontal`: pad 1 = cathode (K, body stripe), pad 2 = anode (A). The footprint includes cathode stripe and K silkscreen markings. J1 is a straight 2.54 mm through-hole 1×6 header, with a rectangular pin-1 pad, individual pin numbers, and a signal legend. J1 pins 1–6 are ROW1, ROW2, ROW3, COL1, COL2, COL3.

Every COL connects switch terminal 1; terminal 2 connects its dedicated diode anode through KEYrc; diode cathode connects ROWr. No pull-ups or controller are added. The 65×65 mm outline has its lower-left at native coordinate (0,65). Native y increases downwards, so switch centres are (12,53), (32,53), (52,53), etc.; measured from lower-left they are the requested Cartesian grid. All diode body centres are 6.5 mm below their respective switches. Traces are 0.30 mm and clearance is constrained to at least 0.25 mm through project rules and an explicit `.kicad_dru` rule.

Validation: final native ERC has 0 errors and 38 library warnings; DRC has 0 errors, 19 library warnings, and 0 unconnected pads. Native schematic parity reports 0 issues. Independent source-side geometry and pin-net checks pass; raw logs are under `../logs/`. Strict warning-inclusive checks are not clean. KiCad 10.0.6 reports the included sym-lib-table/fp-lib-table as UNINITIALIZED and skips them; embedded native symbol and footprint data nevertheless export and validate. Library browsing/relinking may require re-registering the included subset libraries. No further repair was attempted after the permitted two repairs.

No manufacturing or physical tests were performed. Generic tactile switch vendor selection is left open; select a switch matching the documented 6.5×4.5 mm pad arrangement. No GUI or rendered-image visual inspection was performed; SVG exports are available for inspection.

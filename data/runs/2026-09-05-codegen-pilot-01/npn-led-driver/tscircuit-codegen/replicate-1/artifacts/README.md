# Two-channel transistor LED driver

Final entry: `attempt-03/index.circuit.tsx`. Compile with tscircuit 0.0.2462 and Bun 1.3.14: from `attempt-03`, run `tsci build index.circuit.tsx --svgs --pcb-png --disable-parts-engine`.

The editable TSX contains the schematic, PCB placement, footprints and net connections. `attempt-03/dist/index/circuit.json` is the compiled editable schematic and routed PCB representation. `schematic.svg`, `pcb.svg`, and `pcb.png` alongside it are previews. All dimensions are mm.

Q1/Q2 are onsemi MMBT3904LT1G NPN transistors in SOT-23. The manufacturer datasheet (https://www.onsemi.com/download/data-sheet/pdf/mmbt3904lt1-d.pdf, preserved in `../logs/MMBT3904.pdf`) identifies physical pin 1 = base, 2 = emitter, 3 = collector. This is explicitly mapped to numbered pads of the `sot23` footprint, and the compiled port labels were checked. A labelled three-pin schematic box is used for each transistor because the generic tscircuit NPN symbol uses a different pin numbering order (1 collector, 2 base, 3 emitter). The physical footprint mapping is preserved without relying on that symbol's defaults.

J1 is a 1 × 4 through-hole header at 2.54 mm pitch: 1 +5V, 2 GND, 3 CTRL1, 4 CTRL2. Its first pad is square and silkscreen identifies pin 1/+5V and the other signal names (V5 in generated labels means +5V). C1 is a generic 100 nF 0805 ceramic capacitor, located 4 mm from J1 centre. D1 and D2 are generic red 0805 LEDs, pin1 anode and pin2 cathode. R1/R4 are 1 kohm LED resistors; R2/R5 are 4.7 kohm base resistors; R3/R6 are 100 kohm base-emitter resistors. All six resistors are 0805. Individual manufacturer SKUs for passives and LEDs were not selected.

The final board is 45 × 30 mm with two copper layers. D1/D2 centres are (-3, 9) and (12, 9), giving a 15 mm row spacing. Every compiled wire is at least 0.25 mm wide. Autorouter trace clearance and board trace-pad, pad-pad, via-pad and board-edge clearance settings are 0.25 mm. PCB placement reports zero errors and warnings. Netlist, schematic placement, build and Gerber-derived shorts checks returned exit 0. The final schematic placement report contains no placement issues. The route-connectivity metadata audit finds no disconnected nets and no compiled error records.

Limitations: full independent geometric 0.25 mm copper clearance verification was not performed. The open-net audit uses compiled routing metadata, not an independent copper continuity test. React key warnings and chip pin electrical-classification warnings remain in raw logs; transistor electrical behavior was not simulated. Manufacturing exports and physical testing were not performed.

Attempts are immutable: attempt-01 retains a TSX syntax failure; attempt-02 compiles and has no shorts, but automatic group packing moved the LEDs out of the required row; attempt-03 removes group packing, sets explicit clearances and refines schematic dimensions. No further source repairs were made. `package.json` pins the top-level tool version; `tool-package-versions.json` records installed tscircuit package versions. `environment.bun.lock` is the preserved global tool-environment lockfile, not a project-generated lockfile. Raw check logs, scripts, datasheet and result details are in `../logs` and `../result.json`.
